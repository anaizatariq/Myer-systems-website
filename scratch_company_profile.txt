Myer Systems collaborates with healthcare and commercial organisations to offer offshore resource augmentation, medical billing services, and AI system development. The company prioritises delivering dependable support through organised systems, trained experts, and transparent accountability. Headquartered in Dallas with a delivery hub in Lahore, Pakistan, Myer Systems serves clients across North America and beyond.

Services & Industry Focus

Myer Systems operates at the intersection of healthcare technology and enterprise AI, delivering across three core pillars:

Industry SaaS — Scalable, vertically-focused software-as-a-service products built for healthcare and commercial markets.

Enterprise-Grade AI Solutions — Advanced AI systems engineered for accountability, scalability, and control at enterprise scale.

Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) — End-to-end AI-powered RCM covering patient access, coding, billing, collections, and reporting.

Business Reengineering — Eliminating workflow inefficiencies and rebuilding smarter, more predictable operational processes.

Resource Augmentation — Combining AI agents with skilled human expertise to scale operations without compromising quality.

Medical Billing Services — Offshore medical billing and coding services for US-based healthcare organisations.

Myer Systems helps organisations transition from reactive operations to predictable, data-driven performance:
